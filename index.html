2. Learn the Types

Clause
A clause is a group of words that includes a subject and a verb.
  • People enjoy watching movies.
  • She listens to K-pop every day.
Simple Sentence
One subject-verb combination.
  • People enjoy watching movies.
  • My brother plays the guitar.
Compound Sentence
Two simple sentences joined by and, but, or, so.
  • I love romantic films, but my sister prefers action movies.
  • Jack plays games every night, so he often goes to bed late.
Complex Sentence
Two clauses joined by because, although, when, before, after, if, since, while.
  • I go to bed late because I watch a movie.
  • When she listens to music, she feels relaxed.
